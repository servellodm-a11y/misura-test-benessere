# Zinzino Customer Rewards Program — English source review

## Status: do not ingest yet

An official English-language Customer Rewards Program overview and terms PDF was located. However, the PDF is linked from a United Kingdom (EN) customer page and is dated 1 December 2024. The available evidence does not establish that this version is the currently applicable legal terms for customers in Italy.

For that reason, this file records the source and the review points only. It is **not an approved explanation of the Italian programme** and must not be used by NEXTWAY to advise Italian customers until the Italy-specific terms have been confirmed.

## Topics covered by the located English source

The PDF describes:
- two reward levels for earning ZinoCash;
- accumulation of shipping-cost rewards on eligible paid orders;
- a second reward level linked to the seventh paid monthly order;
- redemption restrictions, including single-item orders and full-order payment;
- exclusions for subscription orders, Premier Kit orders and certain other products;
- rules for expiration, returns and transferability.

These details are recorded only as a checklist of topics to validate. They must not be treated as confirmed Italian-market rules.

## Required validation before approval

1. Obtain the current Customer Rewards Program terms applicable to Italy, preferably from the Italian Partner Back Office or an official Italy-specific Zinzino page/document.
2. Confirm the effective date and whether the terms differ from the 1 December 2024 English PDF.
3. Compare each rule: reward levels, qualification order, calculation basis, redemption, excluded products, expiration, returns and transferability.
4. Resolve any conflict between the legal terms and the public explanatory page.
5. Only then prepare the final English customer document and submit it for human approval.
