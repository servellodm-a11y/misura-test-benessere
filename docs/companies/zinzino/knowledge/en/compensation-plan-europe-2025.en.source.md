## Italian reference confirmed by the platform owner

The user confirmed that the Italian PDF stored in the NEXTWAY Library, titled `Piano-Comp..pdf`, is the European compensation-plan reference intended for the 2025 plan, even though its printed footer reads **14/11/2024**.

This Italian PDF is 35 pages long. Its printed date and the official English EU master date (12-02-2025) are not identical. The user confirmation establishes which Italian reference to use, but it does not by itself prove that every table, condition and numeric value in the two files is identical.

## Knowledge Base handling

- Treat the official EU English PDF linked above as the primary English-language source, not as a newly invented translation.
- Treat the user-confirmed Italian PDF as the corresponding Italian reference for NEXTWAY's Italian workspace.
- Before either version is approved for operational answers, perform a page-by-page and table-by-table comparison of all qualification rules, Credits, Personal Customer Points, PCV/MCV/WCV, bonuses, time limits, rank criteria, caps, and glossary definitions.
- If any discrepancy is found, do not merge the values or infer which rule prevails. Record the discrepancy and request confirmation against the currently binding company document.
- Do not paraphrase numerical compensation conditions in a way that changes their meaning.
- Keep the income disclaimer and the no-guarantee language with any customer/partner-facing explanation of earnings.
- Do not treat campaign rules, product prices, local tax treatment, exchange rates or promotional terms as fixed by this plan; verify current country-specific terms separately.

## Income disclaimer

The official plan states that the earnings shown are not necessarily representative of the income a Partner may earn and must not be considered guarantees or projections of actual earnings or profits. Success depends on sales activity, hard work, diligence and leadership. NEXTWAY must not present plan examples or maximum payout tables as typical, expected or guaranteed earnings.
