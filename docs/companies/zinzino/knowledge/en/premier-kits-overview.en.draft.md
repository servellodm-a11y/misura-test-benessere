# Zinzino Premier Kits — Overview (Italy, English)

## Purpose and scope

This document provides a high-level description of the Premier Kits offer for the Italian market, using Zinzino's official English-language Italy storefront. It is intended as customer-facing reference material for NEXTWAY's Knowledge Base.

## What is a Premier Kit?

Zinzino presents Premier Kits as product kits that customers can purchase with a recurring subscription. The company describes the subscription as a way to access discounts across its product range. The exact kit contents, product availability, discount, subscription amount, delivery schedule and applicable conditions depend on the specific offer displayed in the Italian shop at the time of purchase.

## Important distinctions

- A Premier Kit is not the same as an individual product order.
- Some offers include a home test; others do not. The kit description must be checked before explaining what is included.
- Some shop offers may be available without a subscription. Do not assume that every kit has identical subscription conditions.
- Product names, contents, prices, discounts and availability can change. Never quote values from this document as current prices.
- Before giving a customer a quote or explaining cancellation, delivery or renewal terms, check the current offer details and the applicable Italian terms shown by Zinzino at checkout.

## Safe response guidance for NEXTWAY

When asked about a specific Premier Kit:
1. Identify the exact kit and market (Italy).
2. Check the live Italian shop page for current contents and price.
3. Distinguish the initial kit order from the recurring subscription amount.
4. State the delivery cadence and cancellation notice only when confirmed in the current offer terms.
5. If a detail is not shown or cannot be verified, say that it needs confirmation with Zinzino Customer Support or the customer's Independent Partner.

Do not infer subscription conditions from another country, another kit, an older price snapshot or a similar product.
